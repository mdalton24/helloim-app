# Competitors — the facts, for us

**Internal.** Names appear here and nowhere on the site. Mark, 2026-08-27: *"do
a competitor analysis without naming the competitor... to point out why we are
better."*

Everything below was read off their own live pages on 2026-08-27, not recalled.

---

## The one that is closest to us

Positioned as a personal AI operating system with named companions, a shared
workspace and integrations. **Cloud, not local.**

**Their published pricing:**

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Explorer | **Free for 3 days** | Trial requires a payment method |
| Builder | **$90/mo** | Up to **9** companions |
| Architect | **$170/mo** | Up to **19** companions |
| BYO Architect | **$109/mo** | Full platform, **you bring your own inference** |

**The three facts that matter, in order:**

1. **BYO Architect is $109 a month to bring your own model.** You supply the
   inference, you pay their bill anyway. That is the single sharpest contrast
   available to us: we ask you to bring your own account and then charge you
   nothing.
2. **Companions are capped and the cap is the product ladder** — 9, then 19.
   Ours is not a number we sell back to you.
3. **Usage resets every five hours.** Their own FAQ: *"Your usage resets every
   5 hours, so you're never locked out for long... upgrading your tier gives you
   more room."* A cap you hit is an upsell. On ours the limit is your own
   account's, and we do not sit between you and it.

**And they are not local yet.** Their own FAQ: *"A local, license-based version
of Zoey OS is in the works."* In the works. Ours ships today, which is a
statement about the present tense, not a claim about their roadmap.

**What they do better and we should not pretend otherwise:** the site is
finished, the pricing page is clear, and the tiers make the value ladder
obvious. Ours had a fake signup form until tonight.

**Unverifiable:** no public screenshots of the product anywhere, no demo video
("coming soon"), download behind a subscription. So nobody outside has seen the
thing — including us. **We cannot claim our interface is better because we have
not seen theirs.** Do not write a line that implies we have.

## The companion one

A different category wearing similar words: an AI companion with memory,
selfies and group chat. Cloud-hosted, relationship-shaped rather than
work-shaped. Relevant only because it competes on **memory** as a headline
feature.

**What that tells us:** "it remembers you" is table stakes in the category and
is not, on its own, a differentiator. Ours has to be *inspectable* memory —
plain text in your folder that you can read, edit and delete — because that is
the part none of them offer.

## The developer tools

Fast, keyboard-first, well made, and aimed at people who already know what MCP
stands for. They win on polish and on shortcut density. They are not competing
for the person who wants to name an assistant and have it tidy their files.

---

## Where we actually win, and it is a short list

1. **Free.** Today, and that is the whole claim — no trial clock, no payment
   method to start.
2. **Your own account, and we take nothing for it.** The nearest competitor
   charges $109/mo for exactly this arrangement.
3. **It runs on your machine.** Their local version is announced, not shipped.
4. **The memory is plain text in your folder.** Read it, edit it, delete a line
   and it is forgotten. Nobody else offers a memory you can open.
5. **Honest connection states.** Grey, amber, earned green. Every competitor
   checked shows binary connected/not.
6. **No cap we sell you.** No companion count, no five-hour reset.

## What we must NOT say

- **Nothing about their interface.** We have not seen it.
- **No named comparison on the site.** Mark's instruction.
- **No future pricing.** Add-ons — visualiser templates, market dashboards,
  custom business deployments — are real intentions and are **not built**.
  Saying "free forever" would be a promise we have not decided to keep, and
  advertising the add-ons now would be selling something that does not exist.
  The honest present-tense line is **"Free. No trial, no card."**
- **No claim about their roadmap.** "Theirs is coming, ours is here" is fine
  because it is their own published sentence. Anything beyond that is not.
