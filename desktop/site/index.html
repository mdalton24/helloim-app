What it is

A crew, not a chatbot.

You name it. It reads the folder you point it at, does real work in it, and tells you what it did. Nothing is migrated, nothing is synced, and we never get a copy of your folder.

On your files

It opens the folder you choose and works there. What it reads goes to the brain you connect, under your own account — or nowhere but this machine, when that brain is a local model. Nothing is copied to us.

On the brain you choose

The thinking runs on the AI you connect — Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, or a local model — through your own account, not ours. That is the honest trade, and it is why you see what every step cost; with a local model it costs nothing at all.

You watch it

Every file it opens in your folder and every command it runs appears as it happens, in plain words rather than as a wall of shell.

Tested, not assumed

Green has to be earned.

You have had a green light lie to you: it meant a password was saved, not that anything worked, and you found out in the middle of something that mattered. Nothing here turns green until something actually answered.

Grey — nothing set upNo credential, no claim. The bar is not drawn against a limit nobody has measured.
Amber — a key exists, untestedSomething is stored and it has never answered a real request. That is not the same as working, so it is not shown as working.
Green — a real round trip passedIt connected, it answered, and the app names the version and when it last checked.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Do I need my own AI account?

Not necessarily. Connect your own Claude, OpenAI or Gemini account and it runs on that — your prompts go out under your name and your limits, which is why we can show you what each turn actually cost. Prefer to keep everything in-house? Point it at a local model on your own machine and you need no paid cloud account at all.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows today. The installer brings everything with it — you do not need to install a runtime, a package manager or anything else first.

Where does my data go?

Your folder stays on your machine and we never get a copy of it. If you connect a cloud brain, what travels is your request plus the contents of any file the crew has to read to answer it — to that provider, through your own account, the same as if you had pasted them there yourself. If you run a local model, nothing leaves your machine at all. Keys you paste are stored in Windows’ own credential manager, never in a file we write.

Can it do things on my computer?

Only if you turn that on, and only one step at a time. Safe agency is off by default. When it is on, it asks before every action — open an app, follow a link, jump to a Windows settings page — and waits for you to approve that exact step. It is in beta, and it never runs on its own.

What does it cost?

The app is free while it is in early access. Whichever brain you connect, you pay that provider directly for your own usage — Anthropic, OpenAI or Google — and that bill is yours, not ours. Run a local model and there is no usage bill at all.

What happens if I stop using it?

Nothing of yours moves. The folder it worked in is still your folder, and what it remembered is plain text sitting in it.
