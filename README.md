# helloim.ai — the private, on-your-machine AI voice assistant

helloim.ai is a personal AI voice assistant that runs on your machine. It talks,
it listens, and it uses **your** AI key — so your conversations and your key stay yours.

- **Private by design.** Runs locally. Bring your own brain (Claude, OpenAI,
  Gemini, or a local model). Your keys and your words never pass through us.
- **Free to use.** The assistant, its faces and its voices are free.
- **Source-available, so "private" is something you can verify** — not just
  trust. The code is here to read.
- **Windows** today.
